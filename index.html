About

As an Information Systems student at Brigham Young University, I work as an AI Product Engineer in the Tech Transfer Office, where I partner with professors and entrepreneurs to translate high-level research concepts into functional, scalable software products.

I engineer AI-powered web applications that reduced MVP development costs from ~$5,000 to under $500 per project (~90% cost reduction) for early-stage university startups. I decreased average prototype build time by 60%, delivering investor-ready MVPs in 1–3 weeks instead of traditional 2–3 month cycles. Independently, I build and deploy full-stack applications independently, owning 100% of product architecture, development, and deployment.

Previously, I supported 200+ students as a Teaching Assistant for IS 201, answering technical questions and troubleshooting assignments involving VBA, HTML, CSS, Tableau, SQL, and flowchart design.

Outside of academics, I served as a full-time missionary in Salta, Argentina, building trust and tailoring outreach across 3+ diverse regions while developing fluency in Spanish and strengthening leadership and cross-cultural communication skills.
